Meet Keith Schipper

Keith Schipper has nearly two decades of experience in communications, public affairs and politics, having served in senior roles on presidential, U.S. Senate, U.S. House, and statewide campaigns.

Keith served as Communications Director for the Republican National Committee during the 2024 cycle, leading a large team focused on making Joe Biden a one-term president. He went on to launch Hesperia Strategies to help campaigns, nonprofits, and businesses clearly and effectively communicate their message in an increasingly complex media environment. Whether it is with network media such as NBC and Fox, legacy media like The New York Times and Wall Street Journal, local and regional media, or conservative media, he has shaped narratives and stories to win hearts and minds for causes and clients.

Keith brings extensive experience building and maintaining stakeholder relationships and leveraging them to deliver results for clients. He has also designed and executed large-scale voter contact programs that educate constituencies, mobilize supporters, and drive electoral success.

Based in Las Vegas, Keith and his wife are raising their young energetic son. Outside of work, he can usually be found agonizing over the Seattle Seahawks, Seattle Mariners, or University of Washington Huskies—or traveling with his family.
